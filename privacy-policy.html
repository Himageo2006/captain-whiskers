Privacy Policy for Captain Whiskers: Treasure Map

Published by IM NORTHSTAR LABS · Last updated 7 November 2026

This policy explains what happens to your information when you play Captain Whiskers: Treasure Map (package / bundle ID com.northstarlabs.captainwhiskers), published by IM NORTHSTAR LABS, on Android and iOS.

The short version: IM NORTHSTAR LABS does not collect or store any personal information. There are no accounts and no sign-up, and your game progress is saved only on your own device. From version 1.0.5 the game offers optional video ads that you choose to watch for a reward; our advertising partners collect limited device data to show them (see "Advertising"). Optional purchases are handled entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store.

Information we collect

IM NORTHSTAR LABS operates no servers for this game and receives no personal data from it. The game does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or any other personal information. The app contains no analytics and no crash reporting.

Advertising

From version 1.0.5 the game shows optional, reward-based video ads: an ad only plays when you tap a "Watch a video" button to get a reward (for example extra moves, a life, or double coins). There are no pop-up ads between levels. Ads are delivered through Unity LevelPlay (ironSource) mediation, which may request ads from Unity Ads, ironSource Ads, Liftoff (Vungle) and InMobi. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, these partners may collect, under their own privacy policies:

  • device identifiers (on Android, the advertising ID) and basic device information such as model, operating system and language;
  • approximate location derived from your IP address;
  • interactions with ads (for example whether an ad was shown, watched or tapped) and diagnostic data.

We ask our partners to show only ads suitable for all ages. On iPhone and iPad the game never asks to track you and does not use Apple's advertising identifier, so only non-personalised ads are shown. On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads). See the Unity privacy policy, ironSource privacy policy, Liftoff privacy policy and InMobi privacy policy.

Your game progress

Your levels, coins, tools, collections and settings are saved in local storage on your own device. This data never leaves your device and is not visible to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the app permanently deletes it, and we have no copy of it.

Purchases

The game is free to play and offers optional in-app purchases (coin packs, a Starter Pack, a Piggy Bank and the Captain's Pass). Payments are processed entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store under their own terms and privacy policies. IM NORTHSTAR LABS never receives or stores your payment details; the app only receives a confirmation that a purchase succeeded so it can add the item to your game.

Internet access

The game's pictures, videos and sounds are built into the app. When online, the app may download its fonts from Google Fonts and a sound library from Cloudflare's public content network. Like any web request, these services can see your device's IP address; no game data or personal information is sent with these requests. See Google's privacy policy and Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Children

Captain Whiskers is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any information from children.

Data sharing and selling

We do not sell or rent your data, and IM NORTHSTAR LABS holds none of it. The only data that leaves your device is the advertising data described under "Advertising", which goes directly to our advertising partners when an ad is requested.

Data retention

IM NORTHSTAR LABS retains no user data. Save data lives on your device for as long as the app is installed, under your control.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data held about you. Since IM NORTHSTAR LABS holds no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to retrieve or erase. For advertising data, use the links in "Advertising" or reset your advertising ID; for purchase records, contact Google Play or Apple directly.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a revised "last updated" date.
